Emulators

Each emulation refuses to start if a real device of that kind has answered on the bus. Versions start as those of real devices; type another (e.g. 7.16.9300 or 11.1) and press Enter to change it, even while emulating.

The setup is fixed while the telescope runs, except the polar axis knobs (inclination and rotation). To change the mount, the wedge, the starting position or the placement error, switch it off, change them, and switch it on again.

The polar axis really points at the inclination and rotation plus the placement error, as a real mount is never placed perfectly. Inclination and rotation are its adjustment knobs: change them while the telescope runs, and the mount tilts, as CPWI's polar alignment asks.

Seen from above: zenith in the centre, horizon at the edge, rings every 30° of altitude.
